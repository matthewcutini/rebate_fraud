## Access Boundary

- Unity Catalog enforces access to Delta analytics through catalog/schema/table privileges, the governed consumption table, row filters, and column masks.
- Lakebase enforces connection permission, PostgreSQL schema/table privileges, constraints, and trigger-generated history.
- The app must enforce investigator assignment, legal-only visibility, allowed status transitions, and field-level workflow rules on Lakebase rows. Unity Catalog policies do not automatically propagate to these Postgres rows.
